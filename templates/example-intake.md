## Q1

Question: It's 5 years from now and you love your work. Describe what you're actually doing on a typical day - not the job title, the actual activities.

Answer: I am designing training materials, improving onboarding for new employees, updating learning modules, running short workshops, and making complicated information easier for people to use in their daily work.

## Q2

Question: What are you genuinely good at that you never put on your resume or LinkedIn?

Answer: I am very good at calming people down when they feel overwhelmed, turning messy notes into something organized, and explaining technical or confusing processes in a way that feels practical instead of intimidating.

## Q3

Question: What kind of problem makes you excited to come in on a Monday? Any of these? Hands-on/physical, Research/ideas, Helping people, Data/systems, Something creative, Building a business

Answer: Helping people, data and systems, and something creative. I like fixing broken or confusing processes, spotting patterns, and then turning that into a training guide or workflow that actually helps people do their jobs better.

## Q4

Question: What would make you dread going to work, even if the pay was great?

Answer: Aggressive sales pressure, constant cold outreach, chaotic management, or a role where I have to pretend everything is fine while people are confused and unsupported.

## Q5

Question: A year from now, what would make you feel like you actually moved forward?

Answer: I would feel like I moved forward if I landed a stable role in learning, training, onboarding, or instructional design where I am building real experience, strengthening my portfolio, and working on projects that clearly help other people succeed.
