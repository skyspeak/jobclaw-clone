## Q1

Question: It's 5 years from now and you love your work. Describe what you're actually doing on a typical day - not the job title, the actual activities.

Answer: I am helping people build confidence and practical skills, creating workshops and simple training materials, coordinating with community partners, and making programs easier to access and understand.

## Q2

Question: What are you genuinely good at that you never put on your resume or LinkedIn?

Answer: I am good at making people feel comfortable quickly, noticing when someone is confused even if they do not say it directly, and translating complicated instructions into plain language that feels supportive instead of rigid.

## Q3

Question: What kind of problem makes you excited to come in on a Monday? Any of these? Hands-on/physical, Research/ideas, Helping people, Data/systems, Something creative, Building a business

Answer: Helping people and something creative. I get energized by building programs or resources that remove barriers for people, especially when I can turn feedback into something clearer, more welcoming, and more useful.

## Q4

Question: What would make you dread going to work, even if the pay was great?

Answer: High-pressure sales, a competitive culture where people are treated like numbers, or work that feels disconnected from helping anyone in a real way.

## Q5

Question: A year from now, what would make you feel like you actually moved forward?

Answer: I would feel like I moved forward if I had meaningful experience in community-facing training or education work, stronger examples for my portfolio, and a clearer path into mission-driven learning and development roles.
